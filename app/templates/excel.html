2 · Edit in Excel

Change dates, clubs, judges, closing dates, or locations right in the spreadsheet. The downloaded file has an EVENT ID column — leave it alone and your edits will update those same shows. Rows you add without an ID become new shows.

Have the master calendar workbook instead? The Google-Sheet / Excel "ESS Specialty & Supported Entry Calendar" (MONTH/DATES … SWEEPSTAKES/FUTURITY JUDGE) can be uploaded as-is — month rows, second-show rows, and shorthand dates like "4-Sept" are all understood. Because that file's typed years are often left over from the year before, pick the calendar year below and the dates are read against it; you'll see exactly what the site understood before anything is saved.

Making next year's calendar from this year's? Download this year, change the dates to next year, then tick the box below when uploading so every row is treated as a new show. (The Roll a year forward page does the same thing without Excel.)

3 · Upload

Uploads never change anything silently: you get a full preview — including every date or spelling the parser normalized — for confirmation first, rows that match a show already on the calendar are skipped, and the whole upload can be undone in one click afterward.
