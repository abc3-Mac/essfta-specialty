No more hiding rows month by month

With the old spreadsheet, keeping the calendar current meant hiding the upper rows as each month went by. Here that happens by itself: the public list and calendar show upcoming shows only, and a show drops off the day after it ends. Your dashboard always keeps the whole year (under "Past shows"), and the printable page can show the full year any time — for example for the monthly Facebook post.

Adding a single show

Click + Add a show. Host club and first day are the only required fields — you can save with just those and come back later for judges and the closing date. The days of the week are worked out from the dates automatically; never type them. A back-to-back weekend (Show #1 / Show #2) is simply two entries.

Editing a show

Click Edit on any row. Change the dates and the calendar and printable page all update themselves.

Cancel vs Hide{% if user.role == 'admin' %} vs Remove{% endif %}

  • Cancel — the show is off, and you want everyone to know. It stays on the public calendar, struck through and marked CANCELED. Reinstate undoes it.
  • Hide — take it off the public site without changing anything. The show stays right where it is on your dashboard with a gray hidden tag, and Unhide puts it back. Use this for placeholders or not-yet-confirmed dates.
    • {% if user.role == 'admin' %}
  • Remove (admins only) — for shows that shouldn't be on anyone's list at all, e.g. a duplicate created by mistake. It moves to the "Removed shows" section at the bottom of the dashboard, where Restore brings it back. Even Remove is not a true delete — the show and its whole history are kept.

Excel: upload the master calendar, edit shows, or draft next year

The Spreadsheet (Excel) card on the dashboard covers three jobs:

  • Upload the master calendar workbook — the familiar "ESS Specialty & Supported Entry Calendar" file (or an Excel download of the Google Sheet) can be uploaded exactly as it is. Month rows, second-show rows, shorthand dates like "4-Sept", and left-over years from the previous season are all understood, and you are shown exactly what the site read — including every date it corrected — before anything is saved.
  • Edit existing shows — download the calendar's current shows for a year. That file carries an EVENT ID column; change anything in Excel, re-upload, and those same shows are updated, again behind a confirm-first preview with an Undo.
  • Draft next year from this year — download this year, change the dates, tick "treat every row as new" when uploading. (Or skip Excel entirely and use Roll a year forward.)

Ticking several shows at once

Every table on the dashboard has checkboxes (the box in the header row ticks a whole section). Tick some shows, pick an action in the bar above the tables — Hide, Unhide, Cancel, Reinstate{% if user.role == 'admin' %}, Remove, Restore{% endif %}, or Shift dates by N days — and click Apply. Shifting by 7 moves everything one week later and keeps the days of the week. Ticked-show runs are batches too, so they can be undone. Each row also has a Copy → next year button: one click copies that show to the same weekend next year and opens it for adjusting.

Roll a year forward

Most shows run on the same weekend every year. Roll a year forward copies a year's shows into the next year in one step: each show lands on the same weekend and the same day of the week. Host club, show type, location, and superintendent carry over. Judges, closing dates, links, and notes do not — they change every year, so the new shows start blank. The preview shows every proposed show; untick any you don't want. Shows already entered for the new year are skipped automatically, and canceled shows are not copied.

Undoing a bulk mistake

Every bulk run — a bulk hide or unhide, a ticked-shows action, a date shift, a spreadsheet upload, a roll-forward, a copy-to-next-year — is saved as a batch. The page that ran it shows an Undo button immediately afterward, and the Bulk hide / unhide page lists recent batches with their Undo buttons, so you can also undo later. Undo puts every show the batch touched back exactly as it was.

What the public actually sees

The public list and calendar show upcoming shows only — past shows drop off by themselves the day after they end (they're still on your dashboard under "Past shows"). Canceled shows stay visible, struck through. Hidden and removed shows never appear. The printable page shows the whole chosen year. The iCal feed carries scheduled shows only.

Administrator tools

  • Manage editors (/users) — add an editor (the password is generated and shown exactly once — copy it before leaving the page), reset a password, deactivate an account (they can no longer sign in; their shows and history remain).
  • Audit log (/audit) — every sign-in, failed attempt (shaded red), and sign-out, plus a feed of recent show changes with who made them. Failed attempts from an unfamiliar IP in a cluster mean someone is guessing passwords — the built-in rate limit slows them down, but tell Albert.
  • Public past-shows toggle — the "Public site" checkbox on the dashboard decides whether visitors get an "include past shows" option.
  • Show-type colors — the color swatches on the dashboard set each show type's color across the whole site (calendar chips, legends, badges). Default colors puts them back to the standard scheme. Pick darker shades — white text is printed on top of them in several places.
